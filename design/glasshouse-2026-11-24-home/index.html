CleanPath

The order of the screen. Round buttons in the header, a swipeable hero with page dots, the stage name in laurels, a big timer, the next stage with a segmented bar, four round tools, a checklist next to “Relapse” (ours: Had a hard moment), Today’s Mission (ours: Today’s three), and a floating Panic button (ours: Breathe, gold, not red).

Opal

The object is the hero. The glass fills the width in its own lit scene, no card around it, on a stone base like Opal’s gem on its rock. The greenhouse frame behind the lamp is our version of Opal’s cave.

Forest

It grows in real time and you keep what grew. The plant grows with every minute you stay out of feeds; swipe the glass to see your week as a small wood, one tree a day, the dry one included.

Finch

It answers you. Touch the glass and the plant rocks and tells you how far the next stage is; at night it sleeps with fireflies; when hurt it droops. Caring for it is caring for yourself.
