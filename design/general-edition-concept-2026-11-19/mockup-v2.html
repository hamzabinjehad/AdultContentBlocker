Lots of feed today
  • A seed that grows. One new leaf per milestone, so the character literally shows the days you kept.
  • Kind, never guilt. "Dizzy" only describes today's feed time; Pip never wilts, cries or loses leaves.
  • Built for motion. Drawn as simple vector parts (body, sprout, face, arms), ready for Rive or Lottie: blink, breathe, wave, grow.

Why it won't feel like AI

  • No glow, no gradients, no glassmorphism. Flat paper colors, ink outlines and solid offset shadows, like a printed sticker.
  • A real typeface voice. Fraunces (soft serif) for headings, DM Sans for text.
  • Plain, specific words. "22 min on feeds today", not "Unlock your potential".
  • Hand-made character. One consistent mascot with its own rules, not stock 3D renders.
