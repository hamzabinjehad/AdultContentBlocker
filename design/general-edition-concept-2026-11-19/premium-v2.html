Premium, v2: two directions

v1 put gold 3D objects on pastel tiles, which looked busy. Premium apps get their feel from depth and restraint: icons float with their own shadow (no boxes), one big hero object with a soft glow, very few borders, one accent colour (gold), large serif numbers and more empty space. Two ways to do that, shown on Today and Shield.

A · Night gold

Near-black with a warm gold glow from the top, frosted cards, gold gradients on the numbers and switches, and a floating bottom bar. Closest to CleanPath's "premium dark", but warm and calm instead of purple and neon.
