Rules for the new onboarding

Value by screen 9

Protection is on before any education or commitment. Anyone who quits after screen 9 is still protected.

Every question is used

6 questions, each changes a rule or a screen. No age, no frequency, no sexual questions, and no "where did you hear about us" in the flow.

Always know where you are

% bar, act name and back arrow on every screen. Single-choice cards move on when tapped; multi-choice shows "Continue · N picked".

Learning is optional

The five "learn" and five "show" pages become a 3-minute "Why this works" read on Today and in Help. Nobody has to read to get protected.

No pressure tactics

No fake analysis, no countdown discounts, no rating request before value, no shame slides. Plus is offered on day 3, not here.

Islamic edition, same skeleton

Hisn uses the same 3 acts. Its welcome and pledge can carry a verse or hadith, as Unchaind does with Bible verses, with its own wording and look.
