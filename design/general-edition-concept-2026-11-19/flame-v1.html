The day flame

Your day count becomes a flame that grows as the days add up: spark → flame → torch → blaze → bonfire → sun. Each day of the week shows a small flame when it was a kept day (feeds stayed under your daily aim), or an ember when it was rough. A rough day never puts the flame out and never resets your count. That is the line we hold against punishing streaks.

Home-screen widgets. Three sizes: the flame alone (2×2), today's feed time against your aim (2×2), and the flame with the week (4×2). Tapping any of them opens "Your days". They refresh with the existing day widget's schedule, and the flame holds still there.

Where it lives

Today's day card becomes the flame card. Tapping it opens "Your days", and each day there opens the day page you already have. The home-screen widget can show the same flame later.

What counts as kept

Feed time under your daily aim, read from Android's usage record (no new data stored). The aim starts at your setup answer, or 45 minutes, and you can change it. Without usage access, days count but show no flame or ember.

Motion and feel

A slow flicker that respects "Remove animations" (then it holds still), sparks only on Today and on level-up, the celebrate chime and vibration on level-up, and a soft tick when today turns into a kept day.
