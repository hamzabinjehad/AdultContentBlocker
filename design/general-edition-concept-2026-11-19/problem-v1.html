Why it's hard

No bottom to reach

Infinite scroll and autoplay remove the natural pause where you'd decide to stop.

Rewards you can't predict

Most posts are dull, a few are great. Not knowing which one is next keeps the thumb moving, the same pull as a slot machine.

A small pause changes a lot

In a 6-week study, a short breath before opening an app led to 57% fewer opens. The urge often passes in seconds.

What we take from CleanPath

The order (problem → why → fix) and making it about the person. Not the made-up score, the "relapse risk 100%", the brain scare images, the fake reviews or the paywall straight after.

Where it lives

In onboarding after "Your real starting point" (it replaces nothing). The same three pages are in the app under Tools and help → "Why this works", refreshed with the latest week, so it's useful later too, not only for selling.

Honesty rules

Only numbers Android measured. Research claims carry their source, and if a claim can't be sourced it's left out. Without usage access, page 1 uses the answers they gave ("late at night, in bed") instead of numbers.
