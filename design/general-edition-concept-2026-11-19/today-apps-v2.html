Built from what Unrot already has

Rules, waits, limits, quiet hours, the feed list and Android's usage record all exist. This redesign puts them on one page and adds the plan and the preview. There's no new permission, and nothing leaves the phone.

Adult content stays separate

Sites, words and images are always-on layers on the Shield tab, not app rules, so no app sheet can switch them off. Today's "Already on" shows them, with any gap as a one-tap fix.

Build order

1) Your apps page and sheet (replaces Block whole apps) · 2) Today's plan and next-up card · 3) Day 1 starter card · 4) Close all feeds and the mini charts.
