One job per screen

Every screen answers one question: how am I doing (Today), what is on (Shield), am I improving (Progress), who am I doing this for (You). Settings live under You.

Never lie, never shame

Green only when it runs. Numbers only when measured. Day count never resets. No fake reviews, fake user counts or fake urgency, including on the paywall.

Thumb first

The pause and primary actions sit in the bottom third. All touch targets are at least 48 dp. Destructive actions are never the filled button.

Noodle earns its place

Noodle appears when it adds meaning (explain, block, break, milestone, breathe), otherwise it stays small. It can be turned off under You for a quieter app.

Accessible by default

TalkBack reads Noodle's words, not "image". Animations stop with Android's "remove animations". Layouts tested at 200% text and right-to-left.

Private by design

Only the hour of a pause is stored, on the phone. Share cards show the day count only. Plus is sold through Google Play; we never see payment details.

Character and style

The brain drawn here is a placeholder. The real character will be modelled and animated in Blender (or Rive). These screens only fix where it appears, how big it is and what it does: walk in, talk, point, breathe, worry, celebrate.

Character rules (placeholder art)

A brain with sneakers.
Two hemispheres and real-looking folds, a small cerebellum peeking out behind, soft shading underneath and a shine on top. Cartoon gloves, laced sneakers with a tomato sole, eyebrows that move when Noodle explains, and eyes with a green iris and two highlights.

Lots of feed today
  • A brain getting its attention back. That is the whole app in one character, and it fits both adult content and endless feeds.
  • Explains, doesn't lecture. Long-press anything and Noodle walks in and explains it in one or two plain sentences. These are written lines, not an AI chat, so nothing leaves the phone. Android can read them aloud with its built-in voice, in English or Arabic.
  • Kind, never guilt. "Dizzy" only describes today's feed time. Noodle never shrinks, cries or loses progress.
  • Built for motion. Separate parts (body, eyes, mouth, arms, legs) for Rive or Lottie: blink, talk, walk, wave, breathe, celebrate.

Why it won't feel like AI

  • No glow, no gradients, no glassmorphism. Flat paper colors, ink outlines and solid offset shadows, like a printed sticker.
  • A real typeface voice. Fraunces (soft serif) for headings, DM Sans for text.
  • Plain, specific words. "22 min on feeds today", not "Unlock your potential".
  • Hand-made character. One consistent mascot with its own rules, not stock 3D renders or AI art.
