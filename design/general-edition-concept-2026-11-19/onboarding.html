Onboarding v2 · general edition

Picture only, nothing built. v2 takes what the real Unrot onboarding does well (full-colour emotional moments, a chat with the character, quick maths in big numbers, "two paths", a week-by-week plan, a big hold button) and does it honestly. Our app today has up to 39 pages; this is up to 17 screens, about 4 minutes, protected at screen 11. The brain character is placeholder art until the real one is made in Blender.

What we took from Unrot, and what we didn't

PatternUnrotOurs
First screenFull-colour hero with "4.8 · 300K"Full-colour hero, three true facts
Chat with the characterLong scripted chat, slang, guilt lines ("you. can't. stop.")Five short bubbles, name asked inside, tap replies, no guilt
Quick mathsBig red numbers + year of dotsSame big numbers from your own guess, with the formula shown
Two pathsRed "brainrot" vs blue "unrot" with generic claimsYour evenings two ways, built from your apps, hours and reason
Weekly planWeek 1–4 "you won't recognise yourself"Week 1–4 what usually happens, week 1 marked as the hard one
CommitmentFingerprint hold buttonRound hold button with a filling ring; locks nothing
Not takenRatings and user counts, rating request, "the more people use it the smarter it gets", countdown discount, fake reviews, coins to unlock appsNone of these. Plus is offered on day 3, never with a timer.
