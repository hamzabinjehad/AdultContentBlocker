Row 2, sharing: every Sunday report ends in a share card; you choose what's on it, nothing private is shown by default, and each card carries the app's name and link. Where "before" comes from

On setup day Unrot saves one number per measure from the weeks Android already recorded (feed time, opens, first and last scroll, longest scroll). Without enough history, your first week becomes "before", and the page says so.

What sharing needs

Cards are drawn on the phone and shared through Android's own share sheet: no account, no upload. The weekly report needs one quiet notification on Sunday. The invite link and "Do it with a friend" need a small server and a web link, so they come after the share cards.

Build order

1) Before baseline, Today pace card, given-back tiles · 2) Your wins: before → now, moments, bests, heavy week · 3) Sunday report and share cards · 4) Widgets · 5) Tell a friend and the rating ask after a good week · 6) Invite a friend (server).
