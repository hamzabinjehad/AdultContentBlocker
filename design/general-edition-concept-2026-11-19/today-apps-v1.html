One sheet per app. Every way to rein an app in, in one place, firmest last, with the cost of a limit shown in time you get back. Snapchat gets "Close the feed only" because it has a feed. Apps without one just don't show that choice.

From CleanPath, done our way

Its home shows today's state and its blocker gives one place with "+ Add". We take both ideas but skip the reset-to-zero streak, the made-up "missions" and the paywall. Today's plan lists only rules you set, with real numbers.

Fixes on the way

The image check gets its apps in one tap (your browsers), not by sending you to the whole-app list. Suggestions never include Settings, launchers or the Store. "Block whole apps" becomes one option of five instead of a page of its own.

Adult content

Stays its own always-on layer on the Shield tab (sites, words, images), not an app rule, so it can't be switched off by mistake from an app's sheet.
