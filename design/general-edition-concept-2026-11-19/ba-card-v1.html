Before / After card: three directions

The card people post. Each one leads with a single headline in plain words, shows the change so it reads in a second (bars, hours or one big number), and ends with the app name, a QR code and the link, so anyone who sees it can get the app. App names stay off unless you switch them on.

Each also comes square (C shown) for WhatsApp, X and posts. The layout reflows; the headline and the link always stay.

Headline written for you

Picked from your real change, never invented: "Half the scrolling", "My evenings are back" (when the evening drop is biggest), "Mornings are mine" (first scroll much later), or your own words if you typed them.

Only true numbers

Every number on the card comes from Android's record of your days. With under a week of history, the card waits and says why, rather than showing a shaky "−80%".

Gets people in

A small QR code and a short link sit on every card. Someone who sees your story can scan it and land on the app's page. A personal invite code can ride on the link later.
