One job per screen

Every screen answers one question: how am I doing (Today), what is on (Shield), am I improving (Progress), who am I doing this for (You). Settings live under You.

Never lie, never shame

Green only when it runs. Numbers only when measured. Day count never resets. No fake reviews, fake user counts or fake urgency, including on the paywall.

Thumb first

The pause and primary actions sit in the bottom third. All touch targets are at least 48 dp. Destructive actions are never the filled button.

The mark earns its place

the gold mark appears when it adds meaning (explain, block, break, milestone, breathe), otherwise it stays small. It can be turned off under You for a quieter app.

Large text never breaks a screen

Tested at 130% text: 16 of 60 screens need more room. So on every screen the content scrolls, the main button stays pinned at the bottom, and a soft fade shows there's more. The paywall table, All rules and Progress switch to a shorter layout at large text (fewer rows, "Show all").

Try before you build

The "Try it" prototype at the top runs the real order: 17 onboarding screens, then Today, the pause and the tabs. Every tap target in it is a real control in the build.

Accessible by default

TalkBack reads every line, not "image". Animations stop with Android's "remove animations". Layouts tested at 200% text and right-to-left.

Private by design

Only the hour of a pause is stored, on the phone. Share cards show the day count only. Plus is sold through Google Play; we never see payment details.
