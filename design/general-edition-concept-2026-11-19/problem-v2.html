Honest by construction

The guess comes from setup and the numbers from Android. Every research claim has a source chip, and a claim without a source is left out. The aim is called an aim. Without usage access, page 1 uses their answers ("late at night, in bed") instead of numbers.

Adult content

If they picked it, it's always one of the fixes on page 3 and stays on by default. Page 2 never uses scare claims about porn, the opposite of CleanPath's "dopamine hijack" cards.
