The editor. Swipe between the three cards and add one line in your own words: personal posts get far more replies than stats alone. Every line has a switch, app names are off by default, and the link is on by default but can be turned off. Sharing goes through Android's own share sheet, with no account.

Every week, without nagging

A quiet Sunday notification opens the report with the share editor one tap away. After a heavy week the share button steps back and the fix leads instead. Nobody is pushed to share a bad week.

Growth beyond cards

"Tell a friend" (a ready message), the Google Play rating ask shown once right after a good week, and later "Do it with a friend" and invite links with a free month for both once paid plans exist.

Build order

1) Before baseline, given-back hero, today's live line · 2) Your wins · 3) Sunday report, three share cards and the editor · 4) Widgets · 5) Tell a friend and the rating ask · 6) Friends (needs a server).
