Meet Noodle

A brain with sneakers.
Two hemispheres and real-looking folds, a small cerebellum peeking out behind, soft shading underneath and a shine on top. Cartoon gloves, laced sneakers with a tomato sole, eyebrows that move when Noodle explains, and eyes with a green iris and two highlights.

Lots of feed today
  • A brain getting its attention back. That is the whole app in one character, and it fits both adult content and endless feeds.
  • Explains, doesn't lecture. Long-press anything and Noodle walks in and explains it in one or two plain sentences. These are written lines, not an AI chat, so nothing leaves the phone. Android can read them aloud with its built-in voice, in English or Arabic.
  • Kind, never guilt. "Dizzy" only describes today's feed time. Noodle never shrinks, cries or loses progress.
  • Built for motion. Separate parts (body, eyes, mouth, arms, legs) for Rive or Lottie: blink, talk, walk, wave, breathe, celebrate.

Why it won't feel like AI

  • No glow, no gradients, no glassmorphism. Flat paper colors, ink outlines and solid offset shadows, like a printed sticker.
  • A real typeface voice. Fraunces (soft serif) for headings, DM Sans for text.
  • Plain, specific words. "22 min on feeds today", not "Unlock your potential".
  • Hand-made character. One consistent mascot with its own rules, not stock 3D renders or AI art.
