Unrot for Mac · design v3 · 23 Nov 2026

A Mac app, not a phone on a desk

New in v3: the blocking system, with ideas from the Android edition, CleanPath and Opal. v1 put the phone’s screens into a window. v2 starts from how people use a Mac: the app is closed most of the day, work happens in browsers and full-screen apps, and the menu bar, the notch, notifications, Spotlight and widgets are where a Mac app lives. The Glasshouse idea stays. The look is macOS 27: Liquid Glass, SF Pro, system controls, gold as the accent colour.

Mostly invisibleRuns as a menu bar agent from login. The window is for setting things up and looking back, not for living in.
Native firstSidebar, toolbar, inspector, sheets, tables, Settings with ⌘,, menus with shortcuts, light and dark, right-to-left Arabic.
Gentle at the momentThe pause appears over the one window you opened, not the whole screen. Closing is always the easy button.
Facts, kept hereOnly what the Mac measured. No account, no cloud, nothing on a share card about adult content.

The blocking system

One kind of rule, three ways the Mac enforces it, and a choice of how hard each rule is to get around. Ideas taken from the Android edition, CleanPath and Opal are marked; the Mac version of each is our own.

How a rule is enforced on a Mac

The engine is pure logic and is already written and tested (DistractionRules.swift). Each enforcer only asks it one question: “what should happen to this app or page now?”

Your rulesBlock · Daily limit · Wait before opening · Schedule · Focus · Feeds only
Today’s countsMinutes used, opens taken, opens in progress, extra minutes
Decision engineAllow, Wait (seconds, opens left) or Close (why, until when). Focus first, then the strongest block, then a used-up limit, then a wait.
App watcherSees the app in front (NSWorkspace). Shows the wait panel over that app, or hides it behind a closed panel. Counts its minutes.
Browser extensionsSafari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave. Wait page, closed page, feeds only, tab minutes. Rules arrive over the existing bridge.
System filterWhole sites in every app (a Discord link inside Slack too). Already enforces adult sites and blocked apps’ traffic.
The adult layer is not one of these rules. It is always on, in the filter and the extensions, and no rule, strictness or emergency pass can turn it off.

12 · Build order

  1. App shell: SwiftUI NavigationSplitView with inspector, Liquid Glass on macOS 26+, fallback materials on 12–25. Unrot and Hisn as two schemes.
  2. Menu bar agent and login item (SMAppService), MenuBarExtra window.
  3. Usage engine: app in front (NSWorkspace) and feed tab time from the extensions, per day, on disk; tests.
  4. Rule model and decision engine for block, limits, waits, schedules, Focus, feeds only, strictness, emergency pass and the usage ledger — written and tested (DistractionRules.swift). Next: the store, tables and inspector.
  5. Wait panel over app and browser windows; site version in the extensions.
  6. Feeds only rules for YouTube, X, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.
  7. Glass state (grow, crack, gold, break) shared with Android’s rules; hero and menu bar icon.
  8. Breathe full-screen, ⌥⌘B global shortcut.
  9. Focus with notch live view and sapling.
  10. Progress with Swift Charts, heat strip, garden.
  11. Shield page wrapping all of today’s protection screens.
  12. Widgets, controls, App Intents (Spotlight, Shortcuts, Focus filter).
  13. Settings window and first run.
  14. Verify: build, unit tests, both schemes, light/dark/Arabic screenshots on this Mac.
