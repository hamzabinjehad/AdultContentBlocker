Date: 14 November 2026. The owner captured both onboardings on an iPhone (89 screens of
Unchaind, 64 of Unrot); the originals stay outside the repository. These contact sheets show
12 screens each, numbered in order: `unchaind-01.png` … `unchaind-08.png`,
`unrot-01.png` … `unrot-06.png`.

## Taken into Hisn (Android)

- **One question per page, answer cards that move on when tapped**, with a quiet
  "Your answers stay on this phone." under each (Unchaind's quiz, Unrot's "where did you hear").
- **A question whose answer is used**: "Where does it usually start?" puts the matching
  grant first on the next page and says why in one line. The quizzes above ask fourteen
  questions and use them for a fake analysis; Hisn asks one and uses it.
- **Hold to promise** (Unchaind's pledges, Unrot's fingerprint), in the person's own reason,
  saying plainly that it locks nothing. "Not now" is always beside it.

## Left out on purpose

- Questions about frequency, age at first exposure or sexual arousal. They are sensitive,
  they change nothing in Hisn, and Hisn keeps no such record.
- Invented results: "calculating", "37% higher dependency", "you will quit by", "106 days
  a year". Hisn shows only what it measures (see the 13 November competitor pass).
- Shame slides ("it rewires your body", "it separates you from God").
- Rating requests during onboarding, countdown discounts, paywalls, accounts.

## Applied to the product page (15 November 2026)

The page now leads with the app's **block screen** (“One breath first”) rather than a
settings list, because that is the moment the whole product exists for: Unchaind's
calm one-idea screens and Unrot's mascot-led onboarding both make the emotional
moment the hero, not the feature list.

The Hisn companion (the app's own `mascot_wave`) closes the page above the final
download CTA, so the site has the same human guide as the app without importing
Unrot's character, copy, ratings or coin/gamification system. The page stays one
CTA, one short line and three real screens; the long limits list is separate.
