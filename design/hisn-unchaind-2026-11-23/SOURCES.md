# Words from the scholars in Hisn: sources

Researched 23 November 2026. Every line below was read in the book's text online, not taken from a quote site. Page numbers are those of the printed edition the site follows. The English is Hisn's own translation and still needs review by someone qualified, as does every Arabic choice and placement.

Not used: the hadith «النظرة سهم مسموم من سهام إبليس», which Ibn al-Qayyim cites by meaning but which is widely graded weak. Not verified, so also not used: the ten-point list of benefits of lowering the gaze that some sites attribute to الداء والدواء. Its first point («تخليص القلب من ألم الحسرة») could not be located in the Islamweb edition. Hisn uses the nine-point list that was located (الجواب الكافي، ص ١٧٨–١٨٠).

## For the reviewer

- Hadith numbers to double-check: Muslim 2159 (Jarīr), Bukhārī 6309 and Muslim 2747 (the lost camel), the Tirmidhī number for the palm-tree hadith (not given in تزكية النفوس), Abū Dāwūd 4833 (الرجل على دين خليله), Abū Dāwūd 1521 (salat at-tawbah).
- عمر's «حاسب نفسك في الرخاء قبل حساب الشدة» (إغاثة اللهفان ١/ ١٣٤) was read but not used: the editor's note gives it through Jaʿfar b. Burqān from ʿUmar, which is a broken chain.
- The book تزكية النفوس is a compilation: many of its passages are Ibn al-Qayyim's or Ibn Rajab's words. Where the original was read (muḥāsaba in إغاثة اللهفان), Hisn cites the original.

## Tazkiya library (round 9)

Source: the owner's own Ibn al-Qayyim list, `artifacts/ibn-al-qayyim/src/lib/daily-quote.ts` in
github.com/hamzabinjehad/Ibn-Al-Qayyim-Legacy (2,177 quotes, each tied to an edition page of the
reading site ibn-al-qayyim-legacy.vercel.app). `sourcePageNumber` is the printed page of the named
edition (checked: al-Fawāʾid ط عطاءات العلم ص45 matches our `chain` quote).

Filter (`build_iq.py`, rebuilds `iq.js`): tazkiya only.
- Kept books: the inner-life works (الداء والدواء، الفوائد، عدة الصابرين، إغاثة اللهفان، الوابل الصيب، روضة المحبين،
  مفتاح دار السعادة، مسألة السماع، طريق الهجرتين، مدارج السالكين، زاد المعاد، بدائع الفوائد، حادي الأرواح، رسالة إلى أحد إخوانه،
  جلاء الأفهام، الرسالة التبوكية). Fiqh, creed and rebuttal works left out.
- Kept topics: the gaze and desire, sins, repentance, the self and Shayṭān, the heart, patience, time, dhikr, worry.
- Dropped: 76 near-duplicates (one passage inside another), passages over 900 characters, low relevance.
- Result: 457 sayings. Topic tags are automatic (keyword match), so a reviewer should spot-check them.
- Text is shown exactly as in the list; the brother/sister switch no longer touches any quote text.
- Overlap with our 39: khatir, sabr, chain, tark, waqt, shafii appear in the owner's list word for word.

## Turath (round 11)

Source: app.turath.io (Nuqayah). Two parts, 98 new sayings after removing overlaps with the lists above.

**From the books (51).** Full text downloaded from Turath and cut by first and last words
(`tools/turath_book_picks.py`, output `tools/turath_book_picks.json`), so every quote is the printed
text; only footnote markers and the editor's paragraph numbers are removed. Books (Turath id, edition):
- ابن الجوزي، ذم الهوى (6870، ت مصطفى عبد الواحد) and صيد الخاطر (12028، دار القلم، بعناية حسن سويدان)
- ابن رجب، لطائف المعارف (1144، ت طارق عوض الله)، جامع العلوم والحكم (949، ت الفحل)، استنشاق نسيم الأنس (151144)، ذم قسوة القلب (151116)
- ابن قدامة المقدسي، مختصر منهاج القاصدين (98087، دار البيان)
- ابن تيمية، أمراض القلوب وشفاؤها (1512، المطبعة السلفية) — one passage; the edition has no punctuation.
  The source has a digitisation slip «القلبالصحيح»; shown as «الْقلب الصَّحِيح».
- أحمد فريد، تزكية النفوس (10059، دار العقيدة ١٤١٣) — note p.72 reads «لى قوم» in the source (for «على»).

**From Turath's readers (47).** The public "اقتباسات" feed, tag `tazkiyah` (1,000 fetched). Kept only
recognised scholars, scored for this app's topics and likes, then each one was checked against its
page through Turath's page API: 187 of the top 250 matched exactly, 12 near-matches were replaced by
the book's own wording (`orig_user` keeps what the reader typed). 47 hand-picked; list with ids in
`tools/turath_reader_picks.json`. Cards show "chosen by Turath readers" with the like count.

Links: each card opens `app.turath.io/book/{id}?page={position}`. Turath's `page` is the page's
position in the book, not the printed number (e.g. Ṣayd al-Khāṭir printed p.228 = position 360);
the card shows the printed page.

For the reviewer: Ibn Qudāma's chapter on the gaze (p.275) is quoted without its following words
«فإن النظر سهم مسموم», which echo the weak ḥadīth already excluded above.
