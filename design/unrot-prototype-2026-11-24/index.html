Features

The system

Glasshouse

Near-black space, glossy dark pills, a gold lamp over the glass. The default.

Daylight

Warm paper and ink, for phones in light mode. Every word is dark on light, so nothing white gets lost.

Moonlight

Quiet hours: indigo and moonlight. The sprout folds its leaves and sleeps.

The glass

Drawn live, never a painting: the plant grows from time given back, a crack is one dark line with a white edge, gold is the same line filled, broken adds shards.

Objects

One glossy round tile per tool, white mark on dark glass. The same tile on Android and iPhone.

Components

Set it up · 4 minNot now 😴 Sleep🎯 Focus ⛉ 5/5🌱 Day 4

Gold pill for the one main action, dark glass for the rest. Switches follow each phone: round green on iPhone, Material on Android.

Shape and space

132028pill

Icons 13 · cards 20 · sheets 28 · buttons and tabs are pills. Spacing steps of 4: 8 between rows, 12 between cards, 18 at the screen edge.

Motion

The plant only lifts from the soil, slowly (7 s); at night half as fast; hurt, it stays still. A gold seam shimmers every 2.4 s. Screens rise 12 px as they come in. A new stage gets one burst. With Reduce Motion on, all of it stops.

Words

Plain and kind. Numbers are the person's own, never a score. "Nothing is lost" whenever the glass is hurt. Adult content is named only where the person needs it, never on a share card, a widget or a notification.

Type

What stays Unrot

Every protection and tool in today's app (closed feeds, waits, limits, quiet hours, adult sites, words and images, the lock, lists, health) is here, drawn in this style. Only the look changes. Adult-content numbers never show on a share card, a widget or a notification.
