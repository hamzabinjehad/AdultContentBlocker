Date: 20 November 2026. Device: the owner's Samsung SM-G990B2 (Android 16, German system
language). Only screens were viewed: no sign-in, no purchase, no setting changed. A stray tap
opened another app once; those screenshots were deleted.

## Unrot's Progress page today (owner's phone)

- The headline "39 h 37 min" never says which period it covers.
- "44% more than last week" in red is the only comparison.
- Day initials come from the phone's language (S S M D M D F), not the app's.
- No app breakdown and no way into a single day.
- "Today": 0 walked away · 4 pauses · 0 opens, with no history.
- Seven grey milestone boxes take half the page.

## What to take, and where Unrot does better

| From | Take | Unrot's version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opal score page | Compare with **your own** average, on a centred bar; step day by day. | Same idea, but no invented 0–100 score. Rows chosen for the person's goals: first scroll (Mornings), last scroll (Sleep), opens, longest scroll, walked away. |
| Opal | History. | **Free**, every day Android still records. Opal sells it. |
| Opal home | Per-app bars coloured by "distracting". | Colour comes from the person's real rules (a feed, wait or limit), not a separate list to maintain. |
| one sec research | Measure **opens**, not only minutes. | An Opens card, and what happened each time Unrot stepped in: walked away / breathed / opened anyway. |
| Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing | App list with change vs last week; tap a day. | Same, inside Unrot's week card. |
| CleanPath | An explainer sheet for the numbers. | Kept (the "?" already opens it). Its recovery %, levels and mood scores are left out: nothing measures them. |
| Opal streak | A warm line on progress. | Day count that never resets, shown as a compact path to the next milestone. |
