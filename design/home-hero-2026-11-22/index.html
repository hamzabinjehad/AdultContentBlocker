The new Today, working: a sprout under glass

Opal's object-on-a-stone home and CleanPath's named stage with a bar to the next one, rebuilt around Unrot's own sprout. Change the state on the right, drag the time, and tap things inside the phone.

Try it

Moment
Theme
  1. Tap 💧 +8 m beside the bar: yesterday's real time given back pours in, drops fall, and the bar and number grow. A daily ritual (Bloom, Finch), but nothing ever wilts.
  2. Swipe the sprout or tap the shapes at its sides: earlier stages, and the next one as a locked shadow (QUITTR, CleanPath).
  3. Drag Time given back past 1 h: a new stage arrives with a celebration.
  4. Tap the sprout or the number: “Your sprout” opens, like Opal's score page.
  5. Tap the chip at the top right: on Day 1 it says “2 steps left” and opens the setup checklist.
  6. Tap Breathe: the breathing screen. Scroll inside the phone for the rest of Today.

What comes from where

OOpal: full-screen dark scene, one object on a stone, three rounded chips under the number, tap for the why, a floating pill for problems, a celebration card lower down.
CCleanPath: the stage name between laurels, the bar to the next stage, one big named button for the hard moment, a floating tab bar, a full-screen moment when you reach a new stage.
BBloom and Finch: a small daily ritual. Bloom earns a watering can each day you keep your rules; we pour in yesterday's real time given back.
QQUITTR: swipe the hero to see the stages around it, with the next one locked, and four round actions right under the numbers.
UUnrot's own: the sprout from our icon, growth that never resets, dewdrops for today's pauses, the sprout sleeping in quiet hours, honest numbers from this phone only.

Rules it keeps

  • One tool for all three: endless scrolling, adult content, and phone time. Each gets one chip under the sprout and a row on its page.
  • Only real numbers from this phone: time given back, feed time, adult content kept out, phone time, pauses. Nothing guessed.
  • Growth never goes down. A heavy day just grows less (Marlatt and Gordon's abstinence violation effect).
  • Setup starts partly full: onboarding counts (Nunes and Drèze's endowed progress).
  • The next stage is always close enough to see (goal gradient).
  • Drawn in code, so it runs on Android 6 and follows light and dark.
