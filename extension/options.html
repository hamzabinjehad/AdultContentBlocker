Always allowed

Overrides the published domain and address lists. Page-text checks still apply. In Strict mode, these are the sites you can browse. More specific rules win; a block wins a tie.

Content checking

Your content words

Up to 200 words or phrases, one per line, with 4–100 characters each. These add to page-text scoring; common words can also block ordinary pages. Page-text checking must be active.
