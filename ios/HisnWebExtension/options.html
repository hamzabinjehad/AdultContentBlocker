Hisn for Safari

Text checking

Included

Checks page text and individual X posts on-device. Allow Hisn to access the websites you want it to protect in Safari settings.

Experimental visual checks

Face detection is not whole-body detection. Appearance estimates can be wrong and are not adult-content labels. When a face matches, the whole image or video is blurred.

Visual checks are off.

Know the limits

Only readable images and sampled video frames on permitted Safari webpages can be checked. Protected or cross-origin media may be unreadable; unreadable is not the same as safe. Normal videos are not hidden by default.

Models are bundled. Images and frames are processed on this device, never uploaded or saved by Hisn.

These Safari visual settings are separate from the app commitment. Safari can disable the extension; this page does not prevent removal or claim system-wide protection.
