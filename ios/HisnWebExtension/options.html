Protection layers

Choose what Hisn checks and what you prefer to blur. Each layer has its own job.

Allow access in Safari

Required

Hisn can check only the Safari websites you allow. These choices do not grant website access.

How to enable website protection
  1. In Safari, open the Extensions menu and enable Hisn Text.
  2. In Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Hisn Text, review website access and choose Allow for the sites you want protected.
  3. Review access for your Safari profile and Private Browsing too. Return to the webpage after changing access.

Text checking

Included

Checks page text and individual X posts on-device. Text checks are separate from the optional visual choices below.

Adult-media checks

Experimental

Checks readable images and sampled video frames for adult content. This does not depend on a person’s appearance.

Person blur

Optional

A personal viewing preference, not an adult-content label. A matching detected face blurs the whole image or video.

Choose who to blur

Face detection is not whole-body detection. Women/men are appearance estimates, can be wrong, and may miss people. They do not identify a person’s gender or whether content is adult.

Coverage, privacy and limitations

Only readable images and sampled video frames on permitted Safari webpages can be checked. Some frames can be missed. Normal videos are not hidden by default. This extension does not inspect other apps or every iPhone/iPad browser.

Models are bundled. Images and frames are processed on this device, never uploaded or saved by Hisn.

These Safari visual settings are separate from the app commitment. Safari can disable the extension; this page does not prevent removal or claim system-wide protection.
